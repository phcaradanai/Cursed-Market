## Decided
The UI should feel like a game, not a program. Don't use dropdowns. Show only what the current phase or action needs, because too much on screen at once leaves the player unsure where to look.
Give visible feedback for results, such as a notification or modal. Every disabled button needs a hover tooltip that says why it is disabled. Key info, such as the wishlist, should be viewable in every phase, not just at the encounter.
