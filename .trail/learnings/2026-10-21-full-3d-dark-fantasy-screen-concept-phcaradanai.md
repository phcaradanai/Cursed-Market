## Decided
On 2026-10-21 the user requested new 3D dark fantasy concept images for all Cursed Market screens. The exploration is saved at docs/art/concepts/3d-dark-fantasy-2026-10-21/ with a gallery and exact prompts. The 18-screen inventory covers entry, lobby, portals, inspection, appraisal, purchase, event decisions, inventory, player trade, Dark Market, optional brokerage auction, unsold goods, merchant services, statuses/contracts, round income/coin, Collector results, spectators and rules/settings.
## Tried and ruled out
A single overview image would hide the different decision surfaces. Separate full-screen images preserve the phase-by-phase game experience. Generated rulebook ranked-mode/unlock copy was rejected because accounts/ranking/permanent progression are not current gameplay.
## Watch out
These images are concepts, not implemented UI or an approved design system. Text, merchants and numbers generated inside concepts can be illustrative; use canonical copy and rules during implementation. Never expose hidden price ranges before price inspection. Keep existing game changes separate from these concept artifacts.
