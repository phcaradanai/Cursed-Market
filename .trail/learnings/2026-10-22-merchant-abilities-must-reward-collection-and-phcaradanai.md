## Decided
User feedback on 2026-10-22: trust-unlocked merchant abilities must be evaluated against collecting valuable items. Each merchant needs a compelling practical use; thematic variety alone does not establish balance. The assistant's initial eight-merchant proposal was rejected as unbalanced, not approved for implementation.
## Tried and ruled out
The initial Alien stock reroll and Autometa inspection proposals were much more broadly useful than the other abilities. Beast portal information had no demonstrated decision value. Swarm destroying an inventory item for energy opposed collection and became worse for valuable items. Deepsea curse insurance required the player to spend effort inspecting for a curse before claiming compensation. The other initially proposed abilities lacked a distinctive reason to choose them.
## Watch out
Do not treat bigger numbers, equal use limits, or a table of themed abilities as balance proof. Explain when the player would choose each ability over alternatives, what practical benefit follows, and how activation burden and opportunity cost compare. New replacement abilities and numeric trust tiers remain proposals until the user accepts them.
