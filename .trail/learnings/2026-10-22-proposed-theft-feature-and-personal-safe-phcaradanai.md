## Decided
The user requested brainstorming for a theft feature that can target both NPC merchants and other players. The requested personal vault opens after end-of-phase payouts. Deposited items cannot be stolen, cannot be used, and provide no effects to their owner while stored. This is feature planning, not authorization to implement a chosen theft ruleset. The existing state.vault used by Dark Market brokerage is a separate system and should not be conflated with this personal safe vault.

## Tried and ruled out
No theft probabilities, penalties, vault capacity, withdrawal timing, or final scoring rules have been approved. Do not treat brainstorming numbers as established rules.

## Watch out
The current pooled-trust stock rule supersedes the older all-merchants trust rule. A theft penalty that reduces merchant trust may also reduce future shop stock bonuses. Resolve withdrawal timing and deposited-item effect eligibility before implementation.
