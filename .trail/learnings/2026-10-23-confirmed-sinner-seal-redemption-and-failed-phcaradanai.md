## Decided
User explicitly confirmed on2026-10-23: every failed PvP theft gives the defender one sinner seal naming the thief. Any current holder may redeem two seals naming the same thief, including seals bought from other players. Redemption consumes both seals and awards one concealed choice from three randomly presented carried goods of the named thief. Personal vault and loan collateral goods are excluded. The forced2% compensation based on the attempted good's latest transaction price applies only when theft fails, never every attempt; personal vault credits cannot fund it. The prior confirmed cooldown remains two following encounters against the same victim only. Seals may be traded for negotiated credits, energy, life and items. These confirmations resolve the matching pending interpretations in the prior learning.

## Tried and ruled out
Do not limit redemption to the original victim. Do not leave redeemed seals reusable. Do not seize protected vault or collateral goods. Do not charge2% on successful theft. Prior assistant5-credit penalty and global cooldown remain superseded.

## Watch out
Design only; implementation not requested yet. Unresolved: rounding2%, absent last-trade price, insufficient carried credits, retirement of a thief's own bought-back seals, fewer than three or zero eligible goods, and sealed-choice locking. Multi-case criminal notice pricing and personal-vault interest remain unapproved assistant proposals. Preserve concealed target identity and avoid pre-theft fee quotes that reveal hidden transaction price.
