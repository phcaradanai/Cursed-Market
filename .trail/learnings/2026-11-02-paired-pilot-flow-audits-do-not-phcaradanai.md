## Decided
Keep native pilot29 lifecycle proof separate from8 short paired-policy audits. New read-only docs/game-design/scripts/pilot-coverage.mjs verifies original audit SHA256, current pinned game/simulation source, config/seed/player identity and final/Collector score, then reports command/trace/item/economic exposure. The four native policy runs never activate, deposit/expand vault, clear crime, reroll doors or start/answer theft. Cautious inspections are authenticity-only; appraise count alone cannot prove translator was held or caused a change. Do not infer balanced8 effects from replayable3-encounter flow.
## Tried and ruled out
No new simulation was run for this coverage report. Zero proven exposures means missing paired-policy evidence, not failed effects: separate29 unit/lifecycle tests still stand. Generated audit items include unseen stock/copies/ability goods; counts are not encounter offer rates. Repeated PvP transfers count transactions, not unique goods.
## Watch out
Before tuning, exercise each effect with public-view policies and measured eligibility/attempt/applied/economic deltas plus counterfactual/replay. Collector3+2 proposal is docs/game-design/proposals/collector-wishlist-v1.md, still awaiting explicit user approval. Uniform mean pricing hides slot mapping but reveals aggregate value; do not claim zero information. Purchase hooks/energy, trade ban and Collector escrow closure must be explicit. Existing purchase requires NPC stock; retire requires inventory custody. Do not fake shop/player ownership to reuse them.
