# Expansion artwork (v0.6)

The ten original items and six original merchants keep their painted PNGs. Every other kind now has **its own file**
(`item-<kind>.svg`, `npc-<kind>.svg`) so no two names share one picture. These are hand-built vector illustrations,
generated by `scripts/gen-art-items.mjs` and `scripts/gen-art-npcs.mjs`. They are readable placeholders, **not** painterly
renders like the originals.

To replace one with a painted image, drop `item-<kind>.png` into `src/appraisal-client/public/art/` and move the kind into
`PAINTED_KINDS` (items) or `PAINTED_NPCS` (merchants) in `src/game/appraisal/content.ts`.

## Style prompt (matches the originals)

> Photorealistic fantasy product shot on a dark, mottled brown studio backdrop, single object centred and slightly angled,
> soft warm key light, aged bronze and bone materials, fine engraved detail, shallow depth of field, 1254×1254, no text.
> Merchants: three-quarter portrait leaning on a worn wooden counter, 1122×1402, painterly rim light, dark mottled background.
