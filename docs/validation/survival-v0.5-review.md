### F2 — withhold hidden-value history from survival DTOs before the Collector

**P1 · resolved in the focused verdict.** Initial finding: the survival UI said scoring and true-value reveal waited for the Collector, but `appraisalViewFor` projected the complete old encounter history to every player before settlement. Those rows contained `gain` derived from true inventory wealth and `accuracy` derived from the real base value. Its raw player spread also exposed `self.accuracyBonus`, revealing whether an uninspected appraisal was within 10% of the hidden base. This bypassed the private inspection boundary through protocol data, even when the survival UI did not print those fields.

Evidence: `src/game/appraisal/views.ts:33` returns `structuredClone(state.history)` without a survival/result gate; `src/game/appraisal/engine.ts:103`–`:106` computes and stores the true-value gain and appraisal accuracy; `src/game/appraisal/mechanics.ts:88`–`:92` values hidden authenticity/curse/base. Deterministic command-only seed **`history-review-7`**, players `a` and `b`, survival preset: enter available portals; decline optional events; player `a` buys **`item-1-0-0-0`** for **35** without inspection or appraisal; both finish appraisal/trade. At encounter 1 **REVEAL**, `collector` is still absent, but player `b` receives `{playerId:'a',gain:2,accuracy:0,score:0}`. Credits fell from 40 to 5, so the exposed gain reveals the held item's effective true value as **37**, matching its still-uninspected base 37, genuine, uncursed state.

Additional source/DTO evidence: `src/game/appraisal/views.ts:25`–`:29` does not exclude `accuracyBonus` from the player spread; `src/game/appraisal/mechanics.ts:142`–`:146` updates it from hidden base proximity. In a separate command-only branch of the same seed, an uninspected appraisal of 37 yields `self.accuracyBonus:5` during APPRAISAL while the projected stock item's `knowledge.base` and `collector` remain absent.

Bounded fix: withhold or redact value-derived survival history and `self.accuracyBonus` in projections until final `RESULTS`. Keep the private audit/history intact and preserve the exact v0.4 projection. Add a regression using the above command-only scenarios that checks the owner during APPRAISAL and both the owner and another player/spectator through REVEAL and the next encounter; preserve final Collector disclosure and strict audit replay. Do not weaken item-fact projection or remove historical evidence from exported audits.

Focused resolution evidence: `src/game/appraisal/views.ts:29` projects `accuracyBonus:0` and `:33` projects `history:[]` only when survival is active and the phase is not RESULTS. The private server state/audit and v0.4 projection remain intact. The F2 regression at `tests/appraisal-survival.test.ts:185` independently passed both exact `history-review-7` command-only branches: internal accuracy remains 5 while projected accuracy is 0 during APPRAISAL; internal gain remains 2 while owner/peer REVEAL history is empty; history remains empty after advance and in a spectator projection; final Collector still discloses value 37, final history returns and both branches strictly replay.
